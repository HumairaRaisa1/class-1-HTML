Cox's Bazar

Cox’s Bazar is a bustling coastal city and fishing port in southeastern Bangladesh, renowned for its long sandy beach. Located about 150 km south of Chittagong, it is the country's primary tourist capital, blending scenic sea views with towering cliffs and dense, hilly forests.
